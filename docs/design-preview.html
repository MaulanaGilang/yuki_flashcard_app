Yuki · design system v0.1

Every word is a card you collect along the line.

Each language is a metro line, each level a station. Study screens flood with the line's colour, and every word is a collectible card that turns holographic once you've truly learned it.

The live card

Tap the card to flip it. Move your pointer over it to tilt (on the phone this follows the gyroscope). Switch lines and foil tiers below.
